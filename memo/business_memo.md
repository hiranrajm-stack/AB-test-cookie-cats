**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Product Leadership Team  
**FROM:** Product Analytics  
**SUBJECT:** A/B Test Results & Recommendation: Gate Placement (Level 30 vs. Level 40)

### **Recommendation**
Keep the initial progression gate at **Level 30**. Do not deploy the Level 40 gate.

### **Key Evidence**
* **Significant Retention Drop:** Moving the gate to Level 40 caused a statistically significant decline in Day-7 retention from **19.02% to 18.20%** ($p = 0.0016$). 
* **Reliable Downside:** The 95% confidence interval confirms a projected retention loss between **0.31 and 1.33 percentage points**.
* **No Engagement Upside:** Delaying the gate did not drive higher activity; average game rounds played remained virtually unchanged (**52 at Level 30 vs. 51 at Level 40**). 

The Level 30 gate acts as an effective, early resting point that drives long-term player return without dampening initial play volume.

### **Risk / Caveat**
This dataset focuses strictly on retention and engagement; direct in-app purchase (IAP) conversion was not measured. While pushing the gate to Level 40 could theoretically alter 
short-term purchase timing, the guaranteed drop in Day-7 retention poses a severe downside risk to long-term player lifetime value (LTV).
